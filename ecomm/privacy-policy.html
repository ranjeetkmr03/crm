Prakriti Ayurveda ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website or buy from us, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, mobile number, email address, delivery address and PIN code when you place an order, contact us or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Order information: products purchased, order value and payment method. We do not store card details or UPI PINs — payments are processed by our payment partners.
  • Technical information: device type, browser, IP address and pages visited, collected to keep the website secure and working well.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, ship and deliver your orders and send order updates by SMS, WhatsApp or email.
  • To answer your questions and provide consultations you request.
  • To send newsletters and offers — only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations and improve our website and products.

3. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal data. We share only what is necessary with:

  • Courier and logistics partners, to deliver your order.
  • Payment gateways, to process payments securely.
  • Service providers who help us run the website, email and SMS, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Government authorities, when required by law.

4. Cookies & local storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember the items in your cart. We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can clear or block these in your browser settings; some features such as the cart may then not work.

5. Data security

We use reasonable security practices, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and restricted access to personal data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, tax and accounting records). After that it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent for marketing, or nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. Write to us at and we will respond within the time allowed by law.

8. Children's privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent.

9. Health information

If you share health details while asking for a consultation, we use them only to respond to your query and keep them confidential. Information on this website is for general awareness and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
