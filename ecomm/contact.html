Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours and usually arrive in 3–5 working days. Remote PIN codes may take up to 7 days.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes, cash on delivery is available on most PIN codes across India, along with UPI and card payments.

Can I take Ayurvedic medicines with allopathic medicines?

Many people do, but always check with your doctor or a qualified vaidya first, especially for chronic conditions, pregnancy or if you are on regular medication.

What is your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. If an item arrives damaged, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll replace it free of cost.
